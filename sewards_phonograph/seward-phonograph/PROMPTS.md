# Dr. Seward's Phonograph — AI image prompts

Companion artwork to Mina's Typewriter. Dr. John Seward dictated his diary into a phonograph in *Dracula* — capturing audio and preserving it, the parallel to Mina typing transcripts. Uses a **purple / violet / indigo** palette.

**Visual emphasis:** Seward is **recording** into the device (cranking, dictating, sound flowing *in* to the wax cylinder) — not listening to playback.

## Primary prompt

```text
Centered portrait composition of Dr. John Seward inspired by Bram Stoker's Dracula — a composed Victorian man in his thirties, neat dark hair, trimmed beard, dark frock coat and high-collared shirt, calm focused expression. He is actively RECORDING audio into an antique Edison phonograph: seated at the machine, one hand turning the recording crank, the brass horn pointed outward to capture voices from the room, a fresh wax cylinder spinning on the mandrel as sound is etched into it. Deep purple and violet ethereal aura patterns spiral FROM the air INTO the horn and cylinder: luminous filaments, sound-wave ripples, and faint whisper shapes being absorbed and recorded, not emitted. Rich purple and indigo glow behind him like a halo of captured sound being preserved. Dark Victorian medical study background, leather journals, lamp, softly out of focus. Cinematic lighting, painterly digital illustration, recording in progress, no listening to playback, no horn at ear, no horror, elegant scholarly atmosphere.
```
